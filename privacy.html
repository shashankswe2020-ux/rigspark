RigSpark

Privacy

RigSpark has no servers that receive your data. What it knows about your computers and your crew stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud.

Updated 9 November 2026

The RigSpark iPhone app

The iPhone app shows what your crew of AI agents is doing on your Macs, asks you for approvals, and alerts you when something needs you.

  • Where your data goes. Your Macs write their status (names, battery, memory, what each crew member is working on and what waits for you) to the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account. The iPhone app reads it from there. Apple stores it under your Apple ID; we can't see it, and it never passes through a server of ours.
  • On the phone. The app keeps a copy for its widgets, Live Activities and notifications in its own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that copy.
  • Face ID. Approvals use Face ID through iOS. The app only learns whether the check passed; it never sees your face data.
  • Notifications. Alerts are delivered by Apple's push service from your own iCloud. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • Links. Tapping a pull request opens it on GitHub in your browser. The app itself doesn't sign in to GitHub or send anything there.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • No account with us; there is nothing to sign up for.
  • We don't collect, sell or share personal data.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send, with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy. We use them only to fix the app.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from your iPhone. To remove the synced data from iCloud, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone and delete RigSpark's data there.
